## Top findings

1. **U1 (MEDIUM): historical-overlay curves drift one day left for positive-UTC-offset viewers.** `app.js:3328` builds the overlay date via `addDays(...).toISOString().slice(0,10)`: addDays returns local midnight, toISOString reprojects through UTC, and east of Greenwich that lands on the previous calendar day, inflating T by one. Verifier reproduced exactly: T=60 under TZ=Europe/Berlin and Tokyo vs the correct 59 under UTC and New York. It is the only `.toISOString()` in app.js; the actual/projection/marker lines all keep the deliberate local-midnight convention (`app.js:3160-3172`), so the drift is isolated to the dashed historical overlays. Same defect class as the v3 N1 incident, client-side edition. RELATED(N1).
2. **X1 (MEDIUM, latent): the 90-day pace gate publishes a clamp ceiling labeled as live pace.** `0e1c6a1` widened the pace gate from 45 to 90 days (`04d_website_data_v2.py:934`). At 46-90 days out the registration curve sits at roughly 1-2% cumulative (pct@90 ≈ 0.0119), so the ratio scale-up pins at the upper clamp `max(hist)*1.5` (`:948-951`) for nearly any card that crosses 10 registrations there, and the result ships stamped `prediction_source="metadata_pace"` (`:954`, published at `:992`) with no downstream re-damp (walk-in damping skips live-status cards). Verifier proof the clamp binds in-regime: New York State's published `ci_upper=522` equals `max(hist)*1.5` to the digit. Latent today (all 5 current metadata_pace cards are at most 40 days out), and it activates as events enter the window. Landed as a 2-line change inside a UI sweep on the zero-test module. RELATED(G4).
3. **W1 (LOW, filed for the pattern): the mobile redesign inverted a fail-safe.** Four desktop Predictions sections are `<details>` without `open` (`index.html:205,222,238,272`); CSS hides the summary on desktop (`styles.css:3040`), so desktop visibility depends entirely on `syncSectionDisclosure()` (`app.js:5336`) running after three unguarded renders (`:5322-5334`; bare `init()` at `:6053`, no window.onerror anywhere). An exception upstream leaves the sections invisible with no affordance. Verifier downgraded from MEDIUM: it needs an upstream throw first, and such a throw already breaks the surface above it. Filed because the failure direction is backwards: markup-open plus JS-close-on-mobile would degrade to visible content instead (tradeoff: brief flash-expand on mobile before JS runs).

Cross-cutting: the repo's recurring defect class, "one value, two conventions, no reconciliation," showed up twice more in this delta (U1: local-midnight vs UTC date basis; X1: pace label vs clamp reality). The server-side instances were all closed in v3; both new instances are client-side or landed after v3 closed. The second recurring theme is untested-surface growth: every finding in this audit except X6 sits on a surface with zero behavioral tests (G4/G6).
